#4471 merged every "wrong date" report into one ticket and marked it known, with one fix queued. Read by tenant instead of by subject line, the reports describe three different problems. One fix closes only one of them.

The queued parser fix does not close #4471. The form hint and the one-day moves stay broken after it ships.

The problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's date comes out with day and month swapped. This is the bug the queued fix is for.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads what people type day first, the tenant's way. People follow the hint and get the swap. Fixing the parser does not touch it: the fix is the hint, or the form reading what the hint asks for.
  • one-day: the booking moves by one day, not by a swap. One meeting showed different days in two time zones, one month was off by a day, and one invite arrived before its booking. That points at time zones, not at date order. The desk had already read these as one-day moves (desk notes) but left them under parser.

How the reports were placed

The ticket export lists some reports twice. Each report counts once, by its id. Each report goes to a problem by its tenant (locale and booking form, locales.ts), not by intake's tag. Intake guesses from the reporter's browser, and the browser is not where the bug is: a person on a US browser booking with a day-first tenant still hits that tenant's problem.

Lessons

  • Merging on the subject line (decision 0001) put three bugs in one ticket, because every one of them is "the date is wrong" to the person reporting it.
  • Before a big ticket is marked known, read it by where the reports come from, not by what they say.
